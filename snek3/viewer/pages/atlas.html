Three regimes, three panels: a 200M-frame agent, a 100k agent and a 40M-frame 20-game run are not on the same axis. Every number was checked against the paper table its source line names (2026-09-13); grey bars are the reference runs a paper measured alongside its own method, so a panel can be read within one protocol. Human starts, sticky actions and game counts differ between papers, so read differences under a factor of ~1.3 as noise: Rainbow is 223% in its own table, 153% with human starts and 147–150% under Dopamine's sticky actions. Median or mean is the toggle above; hover a bar for the other statistic and the source. Game-specific results (Montezuma's Revenge, Procgen) sit in the table's Atari column instead.

Years are first public release (arXiv or blog), not conference year. LLM post-training methods (RLHF, DPO, GRPO and descendants) are deliberately excluded. 2026 entries come from the RLC 2026 pre-proceedings (RLJ vol. 7); their tiers reflect the novelty of the mechanism, not yet proven influence.
